Who We Are

A Community of
Change Makers

Visionary Minds is a registered Community-Based Organization (CBO) in Kenya dedicated to promoting mental wellbeing, community engagement, personal growth, and social responsibility.

We believe that meaningful change begins when people come together. Our work creates opportunities for people to have important conversations, gain knowledge, build supportive relationships, and contribute to their communities, particularly youth and women.

Our Team

Meet the Leadership

Guided by a committed team passionate about community empowerment and sustainable development.

Elvis Mutugi

Founder

Elvis Mutugi founded Visionary Minds on a simple conviction: the greatest investment we can make is in the development of our own minds. The vision is a fellowship of thinkers, professionals, and dreamers bound by integrity, curiosity, and compassion — guided by the call to "Know Thyself. Master Thyself. Transform the World."

Christine Nkatha

Chairlady

Christine Nkatha is an agricultural and Biosystems Engineer specializing in sustainable agriculture, renewable energy, irrigation systems, and startup business development. She is a passionate advocate for youth empowerment, entrepreneurship, mental health awareness, and environmental conservation.

Ian Kimathi

Treasurer

Ian Kimathi is a certified accountant dedicated to maintaining financial accountability, donor reporting, and the economic health of the organisation.

Baariu Fridah

Secretary

Baariu Fridah is a certified nutritionist with a passion for youth wellbeing, community development, and creating positive change. She brings experience in nutrition, food safety, community outreach, and youth engagement, helping create spaces where young people can connect, learn, and thrive.
