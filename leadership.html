Founders & Leadership

The People Who Started It All

Driven by purpose and rooted in community — meet the founders whose vision and courage gave Visionary Minds its heartbeat.

Elvis Mutugi

Founder

Welcome to The Visionary Minds

I founded this movement on a simple conviction: the greatest investment we can make is in the development of our own minds. In a world overflowing with information but starving for wisdom, I envisioned a community where we cultivate character rather than just chasing success, and acquire the wisdom to steward wealth responsibly.

My vision is to build more than just an organization. I am building a fellowship of thinkers, professionals, and dreamers bound by integrity, curiosity, and compassion. Rooted in the timeless call to "Know Thyself," our philosophy embraces every challenge as an opportunity for growth and grounds us deliberately in the present.

I believe that when we bring enlightened minds together with a shared purpose, we become a powerful force capable of transforming our families, communities, and nations. This is not simply a group; it is a movement dedicated to awakening minds and achieving true intellectual, financial, and spiritual autonomy.

"Know Thyself. Master Thyself. Transform the World."

Founding Vision

"A community where no one is left behind — where knowledge, opportunity, and dignity are available to all."

Nairobi, Kenya

Christine Nkatha

Chairlady

Christine Nkatha is an agricultural and Biosystems Engineer specializing in sustainable agriculture, renewable energy, irrigation systems, and startup business development. She is a passionate advocate for youth empowerment, entrepreneurship, mental health awareness, and environmental conservation.

Her Vision

"A community where young people and women are equipped with knowledge, skills, and confidence to build resilient and thriving futures."

Baariu Fridah

Secretary

Baariu Fridah is a certified nutritionist with a passion for youth wellbeing, community development, and creating positive change. As the Secretary of Visionary Minds, she supports the organization’s coordination and activities while helping create spaces where young people can connect, learn, and thrive. She brings experience in nutrition, food safety, community outreach, and youth engagement, and is passionate about using her skills to serve and strengthen communities.
